How these are selected

Up to four primary yardage projections per position in All, or eight in a selected position, ranked within that position using past out-of-sample error and the width of the forecast range relative to the projected mean. At least four current-season test games are required. Forecasts that pass the market-quality gate rank first. Other forecasts are explicitly labeled research estimates and do not qualify as betting-edge recommendations. This ranking is experimental; it is not an estimated win rate. Ranges describe uncertainty, not guaranteed outcomes. The section refreshes with the latest public model data and respects the player, team, opponent and position filters below.

Clarity starts with the source.

Public data, position-specific models, and results that can be checked.

Stats that match the position.

Quarterbacks: passing volume, completions, yards, touchdowns, interceptions, sacks and rushing. Running backs: carries, yards, efficiency, touchdowns and receiving usage. Wide receivers and tight ends: targets, receptions, yards, touchdowns, air yards, YAC and route metrics when available.

Separate passing, rushing and receiving models use lagged opportunity, efficiency, snap share, team context and opponent results. Each market has its own chronological evaluation and quality gate. More features do not guarantee a better forecast.

Context, without overstatement.

Forecasts assume the player participates. Current availability, trades, weather, specific coverage assignments and sportsbook settlement rules still require review. Offensive snaps are not routes run.

Current route totals inform a separate receiving scenario; they never enter earlier-game tests. Reasoning describes observed inputs, not causal model contributions. No measured betting ROI is claimed.

How the comparison works

Expected value accounts for wins, losses and pushes. A sensitivity check subtracts observed calibration error and sampling uncertainty from the model probability; it is not a guaranteed confidence bound. Research gates check forecast accuracy, input freshness and availability. Manual prices remain unverified.
