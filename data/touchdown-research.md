## Methodology & limitations
- ATTD counts rushing and receiving TDs, excluding QB passing TDs, return TDs and fumble-recovery scores; check individual book rules.
- Distance/type/position expected-TD conversion calibrated only on seasons before the current season, with 30-opportunity smoothing.
- Logistic player-game probability uses prior opportunity, high-value shares, team scoring and opponent-position/RZ context; evaluated by whole current-season weeks.
- Recent volume uses four prior published team games, not an inferred future role; positive xTD minus actual is a regression signal, not a promise.
- Implied team totals are contextual; no arbitrary points/7 TD assumption or fabricated offensive-line grades.
- All recommendations remain research until current odds, availability and model gates are satisfied.
- Feeds contain final stat corrections, not archived pregame data vintages. Missing published players/charting/odds are disclosed.
