# Returning-player workload research

Availability refreshes now build current-team, current-season conditional opportunity allocations for upcoming games. They detect established starters absent from recent published team games, recover forecasts for long-absent returners, and redistribute carries, targets and passing attempts using actual recorded shared-role games. At least two shared games are required; missing player rows are not treated as zero-performance outcomes. Opportunity shares are shrunk toward same-team usage, with six prior team games supplying the opportunity budget. Unsupported active players retain reserved volume. Simulations conserve their allocated team budget.

The browser's central availability adapter applies the allocation to all supported player markets, retaining base per-opportunity efficiency and constraining integer outcomes. Distinct existing ATTD/first/last scorer estimates are repriced from revised touchdown opportunities, preserving first/last game probability budgets. Sportsbook quotes, historical actuals and independent backtest results are unchanged. Unsupported scorer models stay unavailable. Team score forecasts are not arbitrarily multiplied by a player workload ratio.

`edge/return_reports.py` checks official team RSS news and a bounded public indexed-X search. X links must identify an allowlisted reporter and a timestamped status, within seven days. Exact player names and current-team scope are required. Source outages and incomplete indexing are reported in the interface; this is not comprehensive live X access. Existing ESPN injury stories also supply return context. Reports never establish official activation, and questionable/doubtful/IR/out returns remain conditional scenarios rather than invented play probabilities. A return-to-practice report does not imply game availability.

Health snapshots must match the exact base model version and expire after two hours. Per-game kickoff refreshes and the regular availability workflow rebuild the context. The availability workflow also reruns after successful base-model publication. Research notes show before/after opportunity estimates, recorded support and sources.

**Validation:** allocation and consistency checks are tested, but the overlay has not passed a chronological predictive-accuracy benchmark. Adjusted markets cannot become qualified betting recommendations until separately validated. All outcome uncertainty remains; no workload share or bet is guaranteed.
